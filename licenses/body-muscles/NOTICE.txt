This app uses modified versions of the Body Muscles shapes: every muscle shape
was rounded (corner-cutting), the figure was rescaled, left and right shapes
were grouped per muscle, and the colours were changed to the app's own.
The modified shapes are in js/data/gym-anatomy.js.
