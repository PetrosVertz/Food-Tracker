## fridge-a
- Receipts and item photos: only foods and drinks go into the fridge. The AI marks each line as food or not (scissors, paper towels, shampoo, bags…); non-food lines go into a small folded "Not food, skipped" list instead of the review and the fridge, so a wrong guess can be rescued. Barcodes with no food data get the same check.
- Receipt scan, barcodes first: many receipts print each product's barcode (EAN) on its line. Read those and look each one up (your foods by barcode, then Open Food Facts) before matching by name, since a barcode is an exact match. Name matching and the web lookup only cover lines without a barcode.
- Table photo: after every receipt scan, a pop-up offers it (also in the scan menu): "Want it more accurate? Lay your shopping out on a table and snap one photo. I'll combine it with your receipt to get exactly what you bought and how much." Buttons: Take Photo / Skip. The user lays the shopping on a table and takes one photo; the AI identifies the products, reads pack sizes and weights from the packaging, and counts how many of each there are. Use it to fill in what the receipt left out (pack size, variant, brand) and to correct counts and amounts on the matching review cards, marked as changed so the user can check them. Offer the photo by itself only when the receipt read is unsure (lines without a barcode match, unclear pack size or variant, e.g. full-fat vs 0% yogurt), not after every receipt; it stays available as an optional button.
- Say why a value was corrected: a small tag on the review card, "Verified from barcode" or "Verified from grocery photo".
