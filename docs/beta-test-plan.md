# Beta test plan — one month, two testers

Purpose: check that RECOVERY+, ranks, logging and the coaches match what really happens and how it feels. Both testers (Petros and his friend) do the same list. The app keeps its own log (Settings › User Profile (Beta)); at the end of each week tap **Export Profile** and send the file. Write one line per item below in your phone notes ("how it felt") — the app cannot know soreness, so the notes matter as much as the file.

Rules: do the item for real (a normal workout, not a trick), log everything honestly, and do not change settings between items unless the item says so. Note the date and what the app showed *before* you trained.

## Week 1 — baseline (train normally, log everything)
1. Log every meal for 7 days, even bad days. Note the day you skip one.
2. Log every workout with weight and reps. Fill RIR on at least half your sets for 3 workouts, leave it blank for 3 others.
3. Open Recovery+ before each workout and write down what it says for the muscles you are about to train. After the workout write: "felt fresh / fine / still sore".
4. Weigh in on 3 mornings.
5. Log one workout you did not plan (a quick extra). Does the plan and Recovery+ cope?

## Week 2 — the recovery tests
6. **Hit a muscle 2 days in a row** (for example chest Monday and Tuesday). Before the second day read what Recovery+ says. After: was the performance worse than your normal? Write the weights and reps.
7. **Leave a muscle alone for 5–6 days**, then train it. Does Recovery+ say "ready" the whole time? Was the workout better, the same or worse?
8. Train the **same muscle 48 h apart** on a normal week split (push / pull / legs). Compare how it felt with what the app said.
9. **Do 12 sets for one small muscle** (for example side delts) one day, 3 sets another day. Does the app show a clearly longer wait after 12 sets? Does that match how sore you were?
10. **Train to failure** on one exercise (RIR 0) and stay easy (RIR 3+) on the same exercise another week. Compare the wait the app shows.
11. **Train late at night, then early morning** the next day (under 12 h). Note what the app shows.
12. A **bad sleep night** (under 5 h) before a workout: write whether you felt weaker and what readiness said.

## Week 3 — logging and the left / right changes (when they are built)
13. Do a one-arm exercise (Meadows row, hammer curl, lateral raise). Use Left / Right once and compact once. Do the totals look right (40 kg × 12 = 480 kg)?
14. Finish a pair with one tap, then undo it. Are the values restored?
15. Do a drop set on a left / right exercise. Does each drop stay with its own side?
16. Do one exercise with the **right side weaker**. Does the rank show the weaker side?
17. Switch an exercise from Left / Right to compact and back. Nothing should change.
18. Start a workout, close the app, reopen it halfway. Everything should still be there.
19. Edit a finished workout in History (change a weight). Do the profile and rank follow?

## Week 4 — the whole picture
20. Plan next week in Week Planner with **Advanced on**. On Recovery+ the next workout shows ("Gym tomorrow 6:00"). Turn Advanced off: it must disappear.
21. Take **4 days off**, then train. Does Recovery+ show everything ready? Is the first workout back lighter?
22. Ask the Gym Coach: "What should I train tomorrow?" Does the answer agree with Recovery+ and your plan?
23. Log food **only on 3 of 7 days** for one week. Recovery+ should say unknown, not "bad".
24. Add a custom exercise and use it for a week.
25. Go **offline** (airplane mode) for a workout. Everything logs, nothing breaks.
26. After the 30 days: honest verdict. Did the app tell you something useful that you did not already know? Which warning was wrong?

## What the profile file lets me check
- **Who you are:** training frequency, usual time, muscle balance, what you actually use.
- **Is it helping:** strength trend per lift, weeks trained, food vs target, bodyweight.
- **Did the forecasts hold up:** each forecast Recovery+ made, when you really trained that muscle next, and whether that session was worse than your own normal.

Reading the file: indicators show direction only. A good or bad month can have many causes, so I compare the file with your notes before concluding anything.
