## rank (Opus) — new rank engine, icons, bubble, card
1. **New rank score, not one-rep-max only (not Elo).** Today an exercise's rank comes from your best estimated 1RM against body-weight ratios (`tierFor`, `tierBest`, `tierSteps`, `TIER_STD` in gym.js and data/gym.js). Replace the score with one rating per exercise that:
   - is built from **three parts, each compared with the standard for your body weight**, and then mixed with these weights (starting values, the fairness check in item 2 may tune them a little, and the note must say what was used):
     - **Peak strength, 60%.** Your best estimated one-rep max for the lift in the last 12 weeks, from finished normal sets of 12 reps or fewer (the existing `e1rm()`). Weight is the main thing.
     - **Working strength, 25%.** The average estimated max of your **top 3 sets from your best session**. This rewards doing several heavy sets, not one lucky set, so repeated work counts.
     - **Volume, 15%.** The most total kg moved on that lift in one session (sets x reps x kg). It is softened with a square root, so doubling the volume adds about 41% of this part, and it can never beat real strength. This is where reps and total volume count, to a lesser degree.
   - **Body weight is not a weight in the mix, it rescales every part.** Divide each part by body weight to the power of about 0.67 (strength grows with muscle size, not straight with body weight, which is how the well-known powerlifting formulas work). Example: 100 kg for 5 reps on bench is worth about 1.5 times more for a 65 kg lifter than for a 120 kg lifter. Women use the women's standards as today.
   - **Stable without Elo.** The rating uses your **best** sets of the last 12 weeks, not your latest session, so one bad or skipped day can never lower it. It only falls if you stop training that lift for months, and then slowly. A single great set can lift it, but because most of the score needs several heavy sets and volume, one fluke cannot jump more than one level.
   - works for **every exercise that has a standard today** (and for body-weight exercises like pull-ups, where body weight is part of the load), still from finished normal sets only (no warm-ups, drops, right side of L | R),
   - keeps the **15 levels** (5 tiers x 3 levels) and the public names, so nothing that reads `tierFor()` (Profile, know.js, the exercise card, Leaderboards) breaks. Keep `tierFor()`'s result shape (`n`, `tier`, `label`, `next`), add what the bar needs (see item 4).
   Put the rating maths in one place, with a short plain comment on each term, and keep the standards table where it is or move it with a clear name.
2. **Fairness check (write `docs/notes/rank-fairness.md`).** With the new rating, build a table for body weights 55, 65, 80, 100 and 120 kg, men and women, for Bench, Squat and Deadlift: the **kg needed for each of the 15 levels** (one rep and a common set like 5 reps). Check and say clearly in the note:
   - a true beginner in the first months lands in Bronze or low Silver, an average gym-goer around Silver to Gold, a strong lifter Platinum, and Diamond 1 is only for very strong lifters (a few percent) but **achievable** by a real person (list the kg for a 65 kg and an 80 kg man and woman); no level is impossible or trivial,
   - the same relative effort gets the same rank across body weights (65 kg lifting 100 on bench vs 120 kg lifting 100),
   - no jump of more than one level from a single set after the first few sessions, and the rank never falls after one bad session,
   - if anything is unfair or unreachable, adjust the standards and say what changed.
   Add fixtures to `tools/check.js` that assert these properties (monotonic in weight and reps, heavier body weight needs more kg, one bad session does not drop a level, Diamond reachable).
3. **New rank icons, league-style.** Redraw the 5 tier emblems (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) like video-game league badges: a shield or crest with wings or laurels and a gem or star, clearly different shapes per tier (not just colour), with the level (3, 2, 1) shown as small pips or chevrons. Pure SVG line plus a little fill, theme-aware colours that read at >= 4.5:1 in Light and Dark, crisp at 20, 26 and 44 px, in `RANK_ART` / `rankIcon()`. Show the new icons in the Profile rows, the exercise card badge, the bar bubble and the rank lists. List all places that draw a rank in the pull request.
4. **The "how far to the next rank" bubble becomes a bar.** Tapping the rank on an exercise card (`showTierGap`) now shows a small bubble (same style as the info bubbles) with a **bar that fills as you get closer to the next level**, drawn in the **colour of your current tier**, with the **kilograms still needed written once** ("+5 kg", not "50 / 65"). Show current rank on the left end and the next rank on the right end with the small icons. At Diamond 1 the bar is full and says so. Check the bar fills correctly at 0%, 50%, 99% and in Light and Dark.
5. **Warning and rank placement on the exercise card.** Today the red sore-muscle warning sits in the title row and squeezes the title. Move it to the **top-left of the card, above the title, next to the rank badge**: row order from the left is **rank badge, then warning icon**; the gear stays on the right. If only one is switched on in Exercise Settings it sits on the far left; if neither is on, the row is not drawn and takes no space. The title always gets the full width.
